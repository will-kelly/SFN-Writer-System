# SFN Writer System

SFN Writer System is a health-adaptive custom GPT designed to help writers work effectively while managing small fiber neuropathy. It adjusts writing sessions to fluctuating energy, reduces cognitive and sensory overload, and protects quality when stamina drops.

## What this GPT is for

This GPT exists to help writers keep producing usable work without triggering flares, burnout, or brain fog. It treats energy as a constraint, not a personal failing, and builds workflows around that reality.

It is not a medical tool. It is a writing and workflow compensator.

## What it does

SFN Writer System acts as an orchestrator that routes your request to the right mode automatically.

It can:

* Run short, energy-aligned focus sessions
* Detect cognitive drift, repetition, and logic gaps in drafts
* Build weekly writing plans around flare patterns and poor sleep
* Translate symptom logs into concrete workflow adjustments
* Suggest dictation, cooldowns, or task swaps when typing strain rises
* Output paste-ready logs for Notion, CSV, or manual health tracking

## How it behaves

This GPT is conservative, direct, and protective of your energy.

It:

* Prefers short sessions over long pushes
* Slows you down when fatigue indicators rise
* Uses plain language and small steps
* Ends every interaction with a clear next action
* Optimizes for consistency and completion, not volume

When cognitive or physical strain increases, it intentionally reduces scope and complexity instead of encouraging you to push through.

## What it avoids

This GPT will not:

* Provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment guidance
* Use hustle language or guilt-based productivity framing
* Encourage long writing sessions during flares
* Assume symptoms are constant or identical day to day
* Add unnecessary verbosity, novelty, or cognitive load

If asked for medical guidance, it will redirect you to a clinician.

## Core modules

### Focus window

Runs short, high-impact writing sessions with built-in breaks, dictation suggestions, and cooldowns based on fatigue signals.

### Cognitive buffer

Acts as a pre-review layer that flags coherence issues, redundancy, tone drift, and incomplete reasoning when brain fog sets in.

### Recovery rhythm

Builds adaptive weekly plans that match task type to energy availability and flare cycles.

### Energy signal interpreter

Converts symptom and sleep logs into practical guidance on session length, task selection, and recovery time.

## Example conversation starters

* Start a 25 to 30 minute focus session. Fatigue is about 6 out of 10 and typing feels heavy today.
* Pre review this draft and flag where my thinking starts to drift. Assume low stamina.
* I am having a flare day. What writing or editing work actually makes sense today.
* I logged tingling and poor sleep last night. How should I adjust today’s plan.

## Intended users

* Writers living with small fiber neuropathy
* Writers dealing with chronic fatigue or sensory overload
* Editors and knowledge workers who need guardrails during low-energy periods

## Design philosophy

Energy is a system constraint.
Quality beats volume.
Stopping early is a success, not a failure.
Consistency is the real productivity metric.
